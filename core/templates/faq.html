Below are frequently asked questions, you may find the answer for yourself

1 . Are your products really handmade?

Yes. Every item is crafted by hand, not mass-produced. That means each piece is made with attention to detail, care, and just enough personality to avoid looking like it came off a factory conveyor belt.

2 . Will my item look exactly like the photos?

Almost. But not perfectly. Handmade products have natural variations in color, texture, and shape. Those differences are part of the charm, not defects trying to hide.

3 . How long does it take to make and ship an order? nisl?

Most orders are prepared within 2–5 business days. Shipping times depend on your location, but we always move faster than a snail with a to-do list.

4 . Can I request a custom order?

Absolutely. If you have a specific idea, size, or color in mind, contact us. We love making things that don’t already exist.

5 . What if I’m not happy with my order?

If something arrives damaged or not as expected, reach out within 7 days. We’ll make it right. Because handmade should come with human-level customer service.
